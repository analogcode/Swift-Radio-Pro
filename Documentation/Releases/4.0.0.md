Swift Radio’s stations list, Now Playing, station information and About screens have been rebuilt in SwiftUI, preserving the familiar station cards, artwork, marquee titles and popup player.

## What’s new

- Rebuilt navigation, popup presentation and options-sheet transitions, including returning directly to the player after sharing.
- Improved playback and audio-session coordination. Scrubbing resumes playback, while a later Pause, Stop or station change takes precedence over an older seek.
- Shared station and playback state across iPhone and CarPlay, keeping artwork and metadata aligned.
- Loading, retry and pagination support for larger CarPlay catalogs.
- SwiftRadioCore separates playback, catalog loading, artwork and system-media integration from the interface, making the app easier to maintain and extend.
- Swift 6 with complete concurrency checking.
- FRadioPlayer 0.4.0, LNPopupUI 4.0.1 and LNPopupController 4.5.12.
- Updated configuration, architecture and localization documentation, plus a refreshed sample station catalog.

## Also on Android

Our Android equivalent, [Swift Radio Android](https://github.com/fethica/Swift-Radio-Android), uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and the same station JSON format. We’ve added [compose-popupbar](https://github.com/fethica/compose-popupbar) for its expandable Now Playing bar and full player, bringing a similar popup experience to the iOS version’s LNPopupUI and LNPopupController.

## Requirements and upgrading

Requires iOS 17+ and Xcode 26.6+. Existing forks should migrate custom UIKit controllers and coordinators into the SwiftUI structure. The station JSON format remains compatible; branding and content remain configurable through Config, assets and the String Catalog.
